# La Morille Canourgaise

Welcome to the **La Morille Canourgaise** website, dedicated to wild morels from Lozère, their land and the expertise of local foragers.

## Structure

- The French version is located at the project root.
- The English version is located in [`lang/en/`](./).
- The Spanish version is located in [`lang/es/`](../es/).
- Images and logos are grouped in [`logos/`](../../logos/).

## Languages

The **FR / EN / ES** selector is available throughout the site. Each language keeps its own navigation and content.

## Features

- Multilingual navigation
